# GridPilot Product Definition

## 1. Product Vision

GridPilot is a hardware-agnostic energy intelligence and optimisation platform for commercial and industrial energy users.

The initial product converts electricity bills, tariff information and meter data into financial intelligence.

The long-term platform connects financial optimisation with distributed energy asset intelligence, control, flexibility and energy-market participation.

---

## 2. Initial User

The initial target user is a commercial or industrial organisation whose electricity expenditure is material enough for optimisation to matter but which does not have a sophisticated internal energy-management or energy-trading team.

Potential users include:

- hotels
- warehouses
- supermarkets
- cold-storage facilities
- manufacturing facilities
- hospitals
- schools and universities
- office portfolios
- retail chains
- logistics facilities
- data-intensive commercial facilities

Secondary users may include:

- energy consultants
- ESCOs
- solar installers
- battery providers
- facility managers
- energy procurement advisers

---

## 3. Initial Problem

Energy information is fragmented.

Businesses may have:

- monthly electricity bills
- interval meter data
- tariff schedules
- solar inverter data
- battery data
- equipment information
- facility-management systems

But these systems rarely provide a unified financial interpretation.

The first GridPilot question is therefore:

> Where is this facility losing money on electricity, why is it happening, and what can it do about it?

---

## 4. Initial Product Wedge

The lowest-friction entry point is:

**Upload an electricity bill.**

GridPilot should convert it into:

1. structured energy data
2. a reconstructed energy ledger
3. energy KPIs
4. detected anomalies
5. quantified opportunities
6. recommended next actions
7. an evidence-backed report

This creates value before requiring hardware installation or complex integrations.

---

## 5. Product Evolution

### Stage 1 — Bill Intelligence

Inputs:

- electricity bills
- tariff information
- facility metadata

Capabilities:

- bill extraction
- bill normalization
- cost reconstruction
- tariff analysis
- KPI calculation
- anomaly detection
- opportunity identification

---

### Stage 2 — Meter Intelligence

Add:

- 15-minute interval data
- 30-minute interval data
- hourly consumption data

Capabilities:

- load-profile reconstruction
- baseload analysis
- peak detection
- operating-hour analysis
- abnormal-load detection
- load-shifting opportunities

---

### Stage 3 — Financial Optimisation

Add scenario modelling for:

- tariff changes
- peak-demand reduction
- power-factor correction
- load shifting
- energy-efficiency interventions
- solar
- battery storage

Primary output:

**Which intervention creates the best financial outcome?**

---

### Stage 4 — Asset Intelligence

Build a facility energy-asset model.

Potential assets:

- solar PV
- batteries
- HVAC
- refrigeration
- EV chargers
- generators
- industrial machinery
- thermal storage
- controllable loads

GridPilot begins understanding what can physically change energy consumption or generation.

---

### Stage 5 — Optimisation

GridPilot models optimal operation based on:

- tariffs
- demand
- generation
- asset constraints
- weather
- operating requirements
- market prices

---

### Stage 6 — Control

Integrate with compatible asset-control systems.

GridPilot moves from:

**recommend**

to:

**optimise**

to eventually:

**execute**

Human approval and safety constraints remain fundamental.

---

### Stage 7 — Flexibility

Determine:

- flexible capacity
- duration
- availability
- response time
- asset constraints
- financial value

GridPilot begins creating a flexibility profile for each site.

---

### Stage 8 — Aggregation

Combine flexibility across many facilities.

Instead of:

1 facility → 100 kW flexible capacity

GridPilot could eventually coordinate:

10,000 facilities → distributed flexible capacity

---

### Stage 9 — Energy Markets / VPP

Where regulation and market structures permit, aggregated flexibility could participate in:

- demand response
- capacity mechanisms
- balancing markets
- ancillary services
- local flexibility markets
- wholesale energy optimisation

At this stage GridPilot begins functioning as Virtual Power Plant infrastructure.

---

## 6. Product Principle

The long-term moat should not simply be:

**AI reads electricity bills.**

The strategic asset is the progressively improving model of:

Facility → Energy → Tariff → Assets → Constraints → Flexibility → Financial Value

---

## 7. v0.1 Scope

GridPilot v0.1 will focus only on:

**Bill Intelligence**

It should demonstrate:

Electricity Bill  
↓  
Normalized Energy Data  
↓  
Energy Ledger  
↓  
KPIs  
↓  
Anomalies  
↓  
Opportunities  
↓  
Energy Intelligence Report

The objective is not to simulate a complete VPP.

The objective is to build the first trustworthy intelligence layer required to eventually reach one.
